# How the board ranks dishes

The board answers one question: what can I cook with the ingredients I have? It ranks catalog dishes
by ingredient match. Votes, views and your personal history do not reorder the public board.

## Pick your pantry

1. Open **Home**.
2. Pick the ingredients you have. The picker searches the shared catalog in English and Vietnamese.
3. Open the board when your selection is ready.

Your selection is kept in the browser, so a page reload does not lose it.

## What the numbers mean

Each dish card shows `match_count / total_ingredients`.

- `total_ingredients` is how many ingredients the best post of the dish uses.
- `match_count` is how many of those ingredients are in your pantry selection.
- `missing_ingredients` lists the rest, so you can see what one more shopping trip would unlock.

A dish that uses five of your six ingredients ranks above a dish that uses two, even if the second
dish has more votes. When two dishes match the same number, the one with fewer missing ingredients
comes first, then the one with more votes.

## What does not affect the order

- **Votes** change which post is best for a dish, not the board order.
- **Views** are display counts only.
- **Your cooking history** is used only by the Ask assistant to make suggestions more personal.
  The public board stays in ingredient-match order so nobody sees an unexplained reorder.
- **The assistant** never changes the catalog from a suggestion. Adding, editing and voting stay
  clicks you make yourself.

## Why a dish I expected is missing

- The dish uses an ingredient that is not in the catalog under the name you picked.
- The dish has no best post yet, so the board has no ingredient list to compare.
- Your selection is empty. With no ingredients picked, the board falls back to its default order.

If an ingredient is missing from the catalog, add it through the ingredient editor first, then edit
the dish to use it.
